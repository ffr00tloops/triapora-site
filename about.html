About Triapora Security Agency

With over 25 years of service in Metro Manila, Triapora Security Agency is committed to providing professional, disciplined and reliable security personnel. Our mission is to protect people, property and reputations with honesty and integrity.

Our Mission

We build trust through consistent, professional service and continuous training of our personnel. Our approach combines human expertise with modern security technologies.

Our History

Founded in 1999, we have grown from a small local firm to a recognized security provider across Metro Manila. Over the years we have expanded our services and built lasting partnerships with businesses and communities.

Our leadership team is composed of security professionals with decades of combined experience in law enforcement, risk management, and corporate security. We maintain up-to-date certifications in security management, first aid, and emergency response.
